Explainable AI: Grad-CAM Methodology

Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) extracts spatial gradient activations from the final convolutional layer of EfficientNetB0 to ensure clinical transparency.

Multi-Lesion Semantic Mapping

Lesion Type Color Indicator Diagnostic Clinical Relevance
Microaneurysms (MA) ● Crimson Red Early capillary wall outpouching (Grade 1)
Hemorrhages (HE) ● Deep Ruby Red Ruptured deep capillary micro-vessels (Grade 2/3)
Hard Exudates (EX) ● Bright Yellow Lipid & lipoprotein extravasation (Grade 2)
Soft Exudates / Cotton-Wool Spots (SE / CW) ● Light Cyan Nerve fiber layer micro-infarctions (axoplasmic stasis); IDRID uses SE label, MAPLES uses CW (Grade 3)
Neovascularization (NV) ● Vivid Purple Abnormal vessel proliferation (Grade 4)

1. Multi-Class Confusion Matrix (5x5)

Diagonal displays true positives (TP); off-diagonal reflects misclassification
Diagnostic Fidelity
Interpretation: Diagonal cells confirm high fidelity (Grade 0: 81/85, Grade 1: 3/3, Grade 2: 5/6, Grade 3: 20/21, Grade 4: 2/2). Zero catastrophic false negatives (no severe cases misclassified as healthy).

2. Training vs Validation Curves

Identifies model convergence and confirms absence of overfitting
No Overfitting
Convergence Analysis: Both training and validation categorical cross-entropy losses smoothly decrease from ~1.40 down to ~0.20. Minimal generalization gap confirms dropout (0.4 & 0.2) and early stopping at epoch 12 effectively prevented overfitting.

3. Dataset Distribution & Imbalance (PMC+1)

585 fundus photographs across 5 grades; class weighting mitigation
Weighted CE
Class Imbalance Strategy: Grade 0 comprises 72.5% (424) while Grade 1 (17) and Grade 4 (9) represent minority classes. Inverse class weights ($w_j = N / (K \cdot N_j)$) up to $13.8\times$ and dynamic spatial augmentation balance backpropagation updates.
